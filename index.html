What we do

Four kinds of work, done by a dedicated team that learns your product

01 Build

Golden test sets

Realistic inputs for your agent, the correct answer for each, and a rubric for what counts as a pass.

02 Grade

Output review

We grade your agent's real outputs inside your own eval tool and tag every failure by type.

03 Run

Scenario testing

We run your agent through scripted tasks, from browser flows to spreadsheet work, and record where it breaks.

04 Calibrate

AI-grader checks

Two graders label the same cases independently, then we measure where your LLM judge disagrees with them.

Works inside Braintrust, LangSmith, Langfuse, Label Studio or a shared sheet. Your data stays in your tools.

Agents we test

Agents whose outputs have a right answer

We focus on agents where a careful, trained grader can check the work against a correct answer or a clear rubric.

Document and invoice extraction

Typical failures: drops line items on page 2, reads the subtotal as the total, swaps day and month on non-US dates.

We check: every extracted field against a hand-built correct answer.

Scheduling and email agents

Typical failures: books the wrong time zone, misreads what the person asked for, sends a reply in the wrong tone.

We check: each action the agent took, against what the request actually needed.

Voice agents

Typical failures: gives wrong policy details, talks over the caller, misses when to hand off to a person.

We check: recorded calls against your scorecard, and where your AI grader disagrees with us.

Compliance and risk review

Typical failures: closes a real alert as a false positive, or escalates a harmless one.

We check: each decision against an adjudicated answer, with the reason it was right or wrong.

Browser and computer-use agents

Typical failures: stuck on a login or pop-up, clicks the wrong item, reports success on an unfinished task.

We check: scripted tasks run in your sandbox, reviewed step by step from the recording.

Spreadsheet and finance copilots

Typical failures: wrong cell references, hard-coded numbers, a forecast whose statements don't tie out.

We check: formulas and outputs against a correct version of the model.

Graded cases

Can you trust your AI grader?

Your LLM judge agreed with human graders on 43 of 50 invoices (Cohen's kappa 0.61). It was reliable on vendor, date and total. It was not reliable on line items: it marked 5 of the 6 invoices with missing line items as complete, and failed 2 correct outputs.

Recommendations

  1. Extract multi-page invoices page by page and merge, instead of in one pass. This targets 6 of 14 errors.
  2. Add a deterministic check that line items plus tax equal the total. It would catch every total_is_subtotal and most missed_line_items errors.
  3. Don't rely on the LLM judge for line items. Use the arithmetic check above, and keep the judge for vendor, date and total.
  4. Add 10 more non-US invoices to the test set; date and currency errors cluster there.
